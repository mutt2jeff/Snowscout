StashFinder is moving to your phone

The StashFinder web app has been retired. StashFinder is now a free phone app for Android and iPhone, with the same snow scores, map and avalanche forecasts, plus fresh-snow alerts. It's in a closed beta right now.

Sign up to be a tester

Email us with:

  • your phone type (Android or iPhone), and
  • the email address on your Google Play account (Android) or your Apple ID (iPhone).

We'll send you an invitation, usually within a few days.

Email to sign up

Or write to support (at) stashfinder (dot) app.

Already invited?

Android testers: follow the two steps to install StashFinder. iPhone testers get their invitation through Apple's TestFlight app.

Your saved stations from the web app don't carry over: pick them again in the phone app.
