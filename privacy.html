Stashfinder Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

In short: Stashfinder has no accounts and doesn't collect, sell or share your personal information. The sites you save and your settings stay on your phone.

Stashfinder was called SnowScout until November 2026; this policy covers it under either name.

This policy covers the Stashfinder Android app and the Stashfinder web site (mutt2jeff.github.io/Snowscout). Stashfinder shows snow conditions at weather stations so skiers, snowboarders, snowshoers and snowmobilers can see where the best backcountry snow is.

What stays on your device

  • The stations you save, your minimum base, alert and display settings.
  • Recent snow readings, saved so the app works when you're out of signal.

This information never leaves your device and isn't sent to us. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the web site's data in your browser) deletes it. On Android, if you have Google backup turned on, Android may include your settings in your own Google backup.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No location. The app doesn't ask for your location; you pick stations yourself.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking IDs.
  • The Android app has no analytics or crash-reporting services built in.

Where the data comes from

To show conditions, the app downloads public data straight from these services. Like any web request, each one sees your device's IP address and the station location being asked about, and handles it under its own privacy policy. Stashfinder doesn't send them anything about you.

Notifications

If you turn on snow alerts, the app checks your saved stations in the background and shows a notification on your phone. The check runs on your device; no push service or server is involved.

Web site visit counts

The web site (not the Android app) counts visits with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and stores no personal information: it records which screens are viewed and general details such as browser type, screen size and country, so we can see how many people use the site.

Children

Stashfinder is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
