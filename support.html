StashFinder Support

StashFinder shows snow depth, new snow, weather and avalanche forecasts for mountain snow stations in the US, British Columbia and Alberta, and ranks your saved stations so you can see where the best snow is. StashFinder was called SnowScout until November 2026.

Common questions

What is the snow score?

A 0–100 score for each station, based on snow depth (55%), new snow in the last 72 hours (30%) and temperature (15%, colder is better). Stations with less than your minimum base are marked "No go" no matter how cold it is or how much fell recently. You can set your minimum base in Settings. The score is about the snow only: it doesn't include avalanche danger, weather hazards or route conditions.

Why does a station say "no signal" or "last report"?

Automated stations sometimes can't send their readings, often during heavy snow. StashFinder keeps showing the last real reading and says how old it is, and checks the weather service for a winter storm in the area. Missing readings never count as "no snow".

Why do the numbers look wrong at one station?

Station readings are provisional: the agencies that run them haven't checked them yet, and snow-depth sensors sometimes jump or drift. Compare with nearby stations, and treat one odd reading with caution. Some stations only measure snow water (SWE) and have no snow score.

Why is there no avalanche forecast for my station?

Forecasts come from the US avalanche centers (through avalanche.org) and Avalanche Canada. Some stations are outside every forecast area, and some centers don't issue daily ratings early or late in the season. Always check the avalanche center's own site before heading into avalanche terrain.

How many stations can I save?

Up to 25. That keeps fresh-snow alerts readable in a big storm and keeps each update to one small request to the snow data servers. To remove a station, open it and tap Remove from my sites at the bottom of its page.

How do fresh-snow alerts work?

In the phone app, turn on alerts in Settings. StashFinder checks your saved stations in the background every few hours and notifies you when one picks up the amount of new snow you set. The check runs on your phone.

Where did the web version go?

The StashFinder web app was retired in November 2026. StashFinder is now a phone app for Android and iPhone. Saved stations from the web app don't carry over, so pick them again in the phone app.

How do I become a tester?

Email us with your phone type (Android or iPhone) and the email address on your Google Play account or Apple ID, and we'll send you an invitation. Already invited on Android? Follow the two install steps.

Where does the data come from?

Snow data: USDA NRCS SNOTEL, the BC Ministry of Environment's Automated Snow Weather Stations and Alberta Rivers. Forecasts: the National Weather Service and Environment and Climate Change Canada. Avalanche forecasts: avalanche.org and Avalanche Canada. Maps: the US Geological Survey. StashFinder is independent and isn't affiliated with any of them.
