Opens StashFinder in the Play Store app. If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after step 1 and try again.

Open in Play Store

Buttons not working? Tap ⋮ (top right) and choose Open in Chrome or Open in browser, then try again.

Please stay in the test for at least 2 weeks and keep the app installed. Google needs at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row before StashFinder can go public. Questions: support@stashfinder.app.
